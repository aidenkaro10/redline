# Redline: 1-minute demo video

Real product on screen the whole time. No slides. You add zooms and one big caption per shot in editing (CapCut works).

## Before you hit record

1. On your laptop, open https://redline-doiw.onrender.com/signin
2. Pick your friends crew, type the passcode, click Open.
3. Change the address to https://redline-doiw.onrender.com/manager?video=1 and press Enter. The scene buttons only show at this link.
4. Make the browser full screen. Zoom the page to 110% (Cmd and + once).
5. On your phone, scan the "Add your crew" QR code and tap one friend's name (the second one in the list, they're the one who gets hot in scene 3). Keep that phone next to you.
6. Start screen recording (Cmd Shift 5, then "Record Entire Screen").
7. Use the number keys on the keyboard. 1 to 5 jumps to each scene. 0 goes back to normal. No mouse needed.

## Tips

- Do it in one take with the keys, then cut the dead time. Heat (scene 3) needs about 10 real seconds to go red.
- If a scene looks off, press it again. Each press restarts that scene.
- Say the lines over the recording afterward. Easier than talking while clicking.
- The data in video mode is a recorded hot July day, and the dashboard says "Replay" at the top. That's honest, keep it in frame.
